✅ The Green Flags Checklist

52 Signs of High-Quality Women Who Build Real Connection

This is your comprehensive guide to recognizing women worth investing in. Not just avoiding red flags—actively looking for green ones.

The 5 Categories

Category 1: Emotional Maturity (12 Flags)

  • Takes accountability for past relationships
  • Regulates emotions without drama
  • Has healthy boundaries
  • Doesn't need constant validation
  • Can apologize genuinely
  • Comfortable with vulnerability
  • Secure attachment style
  • Handles conflict constructively
  • Has done therapy or inner work
  • Patient and understanding
  • Grateful, not entitled
  • Emotionally independent

Minimum needed: 8 out of 12

Category 2: Communication & Connection (10 Flags)

  • Genuinely curious about you
  • Active listener
  • Can have deep conversations
  • Communicates needs directly
  • Gives you space to be you
  • Responsive and consistent
  • Brings out your best self
  • Can disagree without attacking
  • Shows affection openly
  • Makes you feel safe to be vulnerable

Minimum needed: 7 out of 10

Category 3: Values & Character (15 Flags)

  • Treats service workers with respect
  • Has strong family relationships
  • Financially responsible
  • Supports your goals
  • Takes care of her health
  • Has a growth mindset
  • Loyal to her friends
  • Keeps her word
  • Has her own purpose
  • Humble and self-aware
  • Generous and giving
  • Positive and uplifting energy
  • Values privacy
  • Has integrity
  • Respects your masculinity

Minimum needed: 11 out of 15

Category 4: Relationship-Ready (8 Flags)

  • Fully over her ex
  • Knows what she wants
  • Makes time for you
  • Willing to be exclusive
  • Integrates you into her life
  • Shows effort and investment
  • Communicates about the relationship
  • Values your time and investment

Minimum needed: 6 out of 8

Category 5: Life Direction & Ambition (7 Flags)

  • Has career or purpose she's passionate about
  • Financially independent
  • Growth-oriented
  • Has clear goals and vision
  • Values building together
  • Respects your ambition
  • Brings value to your life

Minimum needed: 5 out of 7

The Scoring System

🟢 TIER 1: High-Quality (45-52 flags)

Wife material. Exceeds thresholds in all categories. 1 in 100. Invest fully.

🟡 TIER 2: Solid Potential (37-44 flags)

Good foundation. Meets most thresholds. Continue dating intentionally.

🟠 TIER 3: Borderline (30-36 flags)

Right at threshold. Proceed with caution. Needs growth. Reassess in 2-3 months.

🔴 TIER 4: Not Worth It (Below 30 flags)

Falling short in too many areas. Walk away. Your time is too valuable.

Standards Protect Your Peace

High-value men filter harder, not faster. Use this checklist to find quality. Don't settle for less.
